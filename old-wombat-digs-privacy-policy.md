# Old Wombat Digs: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Old Wombat Digs is a puzzle game for Android phones and tablets and for iPhone and iPad, made by Matanya Asis, trading as As Is ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the information described here. Contact: matanya.asis288@gmail.com.

The short version: the game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob between some levels, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns the ads off. Your progress is saved only on your own device. The game has no accounts and no analytics of its own, and we don't keep any personal information about you.

## What the game saves

- **Your game progress.** The levels you've finished, your best number of swipes and stars for each level, your settings (such as sound and vibration) and whether you bought "Remove ads" are saved on your device, so the game remembers where you were. This never leaves your device through the game, and it says nothing about who you are.
- **Device backup.** If device backup is on (Android backup with your Google account, or iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad), the backup may include this small save file. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under your own account, not by us, and we can't see it.
- **Deleting it.** "Reset progress" in the game's Settings clears your progress. Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved on the device.

## Ads

The game shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob between some levels: none on the first ten levels, and after that one every few levels you finish or fail. There are no ads while you are digging.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's AdMob service may collect from your device:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- your IP address and the approximate location derived from it,
- information about the ads you see and how you interact with them,
- diagnostic and performance information about the ad service.

Google handles this information as described in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services": https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. We don't receive this information and can't link it to you.

**Your choices**

- Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time from "Privacy settings" in the game's Settings.
- On iPhone and iPad, the game asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency) before any personalized ads; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", you only see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
- The one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns the ads off.

## Purchases

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, or on iPhone and iPad by the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only learns from the store whether the purchase belongs to your store account, so it can turn the ads off, also on another device or after a new install ("Restore purchases" in Settings). Your purchase is covered by the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) or Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The purchase only removes ads; it never opens or skips levels.

## What the game doesn't do

- **No account.** The game never asks for a name, an email address, an age, a photo or any other personal detail, and there is nothing to type in.
- **No analytics of our own.** Apart from Google's ad service and the store's purchase service described above, the game contains no analytics, crash-reporting or social-network software.
- **No permissions for your personal things.** The game doesn't use your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notifications. It uses the internet only for the ads and the purchase.

## The link to this page

"Privacy policy" in the game's Settings opens this page in your device's web browser. The page is hosted on GitHub, and the game adds nothing about you to the link. As with any website, GitHub may keep technical logs of visits (such as IP addresses), as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Children

Old Wombat Digs is a puzzle game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no chat, no accounts and no way to share anything from the game. Ads are limited to those Google rates as suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG) or milder.

Parents and guardians: if you have a question, or think a child's information reached us, email matanya.asis288@gmail.com and we'll help.

## How long information is kept

We don't collect or keep any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game. Information Google collects through ads is kept as described in the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

## Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Personalized ads are shown only with your consent, which you can change at any time from "Privacy settings"; otherwise ads are based on Google's legitimate interests and limited data as Google describes. Google may process information outside your country, under its own data transfer safeguards (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can still contact us about any privacy question, and if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland you can also complain to your local data protection authority. For information Google collects through ads, you can use Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com and the rights described in the Google Privacy Policy.

# Old Wombat Digs: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Old Wombat Digs is a puzzle game for Android phones and tablets and for iPhone and iPad, made by Matanya Asis, trading as As Is ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the information described here. Contact: matanya.asis288@gmail.com.

The short version: **the game collects nothing.** It has no ads, no purchases, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't use the internet. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

## What the game saves

- **Your game progress.** The levels you've finished, your best number of swipes and stars for each level, and your settings (such as sound and vibration) are saved on your device, so the game remembers where you were. This never leaves your device through the game, and it says nothing about who you are.
- **Device backup.** If device backup is on (Android backup with your Google account, or iCloud backup on iPhone and iPad), the backup may include this small save file. That backup is handled by Google or Apple under your own account, not by us, and we can't see it.
- **Deleting it.** "Reset progress" in the game's Settings clears your progress. Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved on the device.

## What the game doesn't do

- **No account.** The game never asks for a name, an email address, an age, a photo or any other personal detail, and there is nothing to type in.
- **No ads and no purchases.** The game shows no ads and sells nothing.
- **No analytics, no tracking.** The game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-network software, and it doesn't read your advertising ID or any other device identifier.
- **No internet.** The game sends and receives nothing over the internet. On Android it doesn't even ask for the internet permission.
- **No permissions.** The game doesn't use your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or notifications.

## The link to this page

"Privacy policy" in the game's Settings opens this page in your device's web browser. The page is hosted on GitHub, and the game adds nothing about you to the link. As with any website, GitHub may keep technical logs of visits (such as IP addresses), as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Children

Old Wombat Digs is a puzzle game for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. In fact the game collects no personal information from anyone, whatever their age: there are no ads, no purchases, no chat and no way to share anything from the game.

Parents and guardians: if you have a question, or think a child's information reached us, email matanya.asis288@gmail.com and we'll help.

## How long information is kept

We don't collect or keep any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the game.

## Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can still contact us about any privacy question, and if you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Changes

If the game changes (for example, if ads or a purchase are added later), this policy will be updated first, and the new version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
